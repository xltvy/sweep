Support

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea? Email sweep.app.support@gmail.com. Including your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Deleted photos still take up space

Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and keep using space for up to 30 days. To free the space now, open Photos, go to Recently Deleted, and delete them there.

I deleted something by mistake

Open Photos, go to Recently Deleted, choose the photo and tap Recover. Items stay there for up to 30 days.

Sweep only sees some of my photos

You may have given Sweep access to selected photos only. Go to Settings > Apps > Sweep > Photos and choose Full Access.

Restoring Unlock Sweep

If you bought Unlock Sweep before, on this or another iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account, open Sweep, go to the Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
