Sweep

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: Sweep collects no data. Your photos, videos and everything about them stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, shared or sold.

Your photo library

Sweep asks for access to your photo library so it can find your largest photos and videos and group near-identical shots. All of this happens on your iPhone. Sweep never uploads, copies or shares your photos or videos, and never downloads originals stored in iCloud.

Photos and videos are only deleted when you choose to, and iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can restore them for up to 30 days.

What is stored on your iPhone

To avoid rescanning everything each time, Sweep keeps a small file on your iPhone with identifiers, dates and sizes of your photos and how similar they are to one another. It contains no images. It is not included in backups and is deleted when you delete the app.

Sweep also keeps a count of the free deletions you have used, and which photos it has shown you without blur, in your iPhone's Keychain. This stays on your iPhone and is not synced or shared.

Purchases

Unlock Sweep is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Sweep never receives your payment details, name or email address. Sweep checks on your iPhone whether the purchase has been made. Apple's own handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What Sweep does not do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that does these things.
  • No connection to servers run by us or anyone else.
  • No sale or sharing of any data, because none is collected.

Children

Sweep collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Sweep ever starts collecting any data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
